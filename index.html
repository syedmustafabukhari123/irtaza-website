How Your Custom Kit Is Made

Work directly with our Sialkot team to turn your colours, crest and roster into production-ready sportswear. Every order is confirmed with you before it goes into production.

01

Share Your Brief

Tell us the sport, garment, quantity, colours and delivery destination. Send a sketch, reference image or tech pack if you have one.

02

Review Your Free Mockup

Our design desk prepares a digital mockup within 24 hours, showing your branding and requested details for review.

03

Confirm the Details

Approve the artwork, sizes, fabric and finishing details. We confirm the written quotation and production plan with you.

04

Sample or Production

Request a physical sample, typically ready in 5–7 days, or proceed to bulk production after approving the final specifications.

05

Quality Check & Dispatch

Your order is checked before dispatch. Bulk production typically takes 15–25 business days after approval; shipping time depends on destination.
